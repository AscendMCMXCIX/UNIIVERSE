## 2. The one behaviour every tooltip should share

1. **One component.** `InfoTip` for every explanation, including chart readouts' look. A `title` attribute only for (a) the name of an icon-only control (which also has its `aria-label`) and (b) the whole text of a value shown truncated. Never an explanation in a `title`.
2. **Short by rule.** One sentence, at most about 25 words (hard limit 40), checked by a unit test like `design-tokens.test.ts` (count words in every `InfoTip text`, `title` and `hint`). Anything longer is visible text under the heading, or a "How this is counted" sheet (the app's `DetailModal`), never a scrolling bubble.
3. **Trigger.** An ⓘ (16 px mark, 24 px target, 44 px on touch) or a term with a dotted underline; always a tab stop with a specific name ("About KDA"), never "More information"; never a `title` on the same element or its parent.
4. **Desktop.** Opens after ~150 ms of hover (at once on keyboard focus); stays open while the pointer is over the ⓘ or the bubble (the bubble takes the pointer and the 6 px gap is bridged: WCAG 1.4.13 "hoverable"); closes 100 ms after the pointer leaves both, on blur, or on Escape (bubble only).
5. **Touch.** A tap opens it; a tap on the ⓘ, on the bubble or anywhere else closes it, and that closing tap does nothing else; scrolling the page closes it.
6. **Placement.** Above the trigger, else below, clamped 8 px inside the screen; never over its own trigger. If neither side has room (large text), it opens as a small bottom sheet with the same text instead.
7. **Look.** As today: `bg-bg-elevated`, `border-border-strong`, `rounded-md`, `text-xs`, `max-w-[18rem]`, `shadow-lg`, `z-[100]`, a 100 ms fade with `motion-safe`.

**Dismissal and input checks** (`pw/dismiss.mjs`, `pw/tapthrough.mjs`):

- Mouse, 1440 × 900: every bubble opened on hover (no delay: 32 ms from the pointer's move to the bubble), closed on leave, opened on keyboard focus, closed on Escape; in the Team window Escape closed the bubble and left the window open. Moving the pointer from the Recent form ⓘ up into its bubble **closed it** (the bubble ignores the pointer and a 6 px gap separates it from the ⓘ).
- Touch, 375 × 667 and 320 × 568: a tap opened each bubble; a second tap on the ⓘ closed it; a tap on empty page closed it; a tap on the bubble closed it.
- **Tap-through**, 320 × 568 at 200 %: with the Ranked progress bubble covering the top of the screen, a tap on the bubble closed it **and switched the queue** (the "Solo/Duo" link under it; the address gained `#ranked-progress`). On Team → Matches the same tap closed the bubble **and opened the Filters sheet**. The bubble is `pointer-events: none` on `main`/3871, so a reader's tap on the text it covers lands on whatever is under it.
- The LP tip on a match card is `tabIndex={-1}` (`match-card.tsx` 163): a keyboard user can never open it; only a screen reader hears its reason (as the card's description).
- At 200 % in the Team window on a 320 px phone, the pool tile's stats line ("2.3 KDA · 7.5 CS") overlaps the middle of the experience label that is its tooltip trigger: a tap in the label's middle hits the stats (elementFromPoint), so the tip did not open.
